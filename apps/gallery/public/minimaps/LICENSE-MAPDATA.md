FlashFront modifications: terrain-derived spawn scores, coordinates and reasons, and
resized minimaps recolored with the FlashFront palette. The generated spots.json and
heatmaps.json (four-tile coast/room grids, quantized and gzip-compressed) and minimap PNGs are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Community notes are short paraphrases
with separate source metadata. These heuristic suggestions are not win-rate guarantees.
